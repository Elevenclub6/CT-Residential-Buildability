About this tool

This is an early-stage screening tool for identifying Connecticut municipalities that deserve deeper residential-development research.

What the score means

The 0–100 score reacts to your selected priorities. It is not a finding that land is buildable, appropriately zoned, available, or financially viable.

Required due diligence

Confirm parcels, zoning, wetlands and watercourses, flood hazards, frontage, utilities, soils, title restrictions and municipal approvals with authoritative sources and qualified professionals.
